## Opportunity
Parcel No. 5223 is an unprogrammed plot owned by the Fondation Versoix-Samuel May, frozen by conflicting interests between nature reclaiming the land, construction crews using it for free storage, and local residents.

## Strategy
Where screens capture visual attention, the sense of smell snaps people back into their physical surroundings. We turned our kitchen into a smell laboratory. Moving beyond visual site conditions, we use scents to reveal hidden layers of the land, bringing artists and neighbours together to interact directly with the space.

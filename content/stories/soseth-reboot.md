Reboot is a generous word for what we did. We took something that had stopped working, asked a lot of questions about why, and then carried it outside.

Most of the week was spent building the thing that would hold the other thing. This is usually the case and it is never in the photographs.

Then it ran, in the dark, with a cable that we all agreed not to look at too closely.

The reason to do it again is not the object. It is the twelve people who now know how it is made, and can make the next one without us.
